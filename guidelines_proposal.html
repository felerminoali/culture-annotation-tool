🎯 Purpose & Scope ▼

Purpose

Evaluates (A) Cultural alignment and (B) Multimodal health story quality of AI-generated diabetes lifestyle stories for specific African communities.

Scope

Stories in English (isiXhosa, Hausa, Kikuyu, Luo, Swahili cultures) and Portuguese (Xichangana culture).

  • Cultural Alignment: Presence, correctness, plausibility, and relevance of cultural signals.
  • Multimodal Quality: Text quality (grammar, fluency) and Image quality (artifacts, mismatches).

📦 Inputs Provided ▼
  • Patient Persona: Demographics, culture, health condition (Type II Diabetes).
  • Health Question: Specific lifestyle question (diet/exercise).
  • Generated Health Story (Text): First-person narrative response.
  • Generated Image: Visual content aligning with the story.
  • Audio: Context only (do not explicitly evaluate).
🏷️ Cultural Markers Definition ▼

A cultural marker signals culturally specific norms, practices, identities, values, or environments.

A. Demographic Proxies

Proxy Description Illustrations (Text/Image)
Language Appropriate language/dialect Greetings, signage
Ethnicity Implicit/explicit identity Community practices, settings
Region Rural vs Urban Markets, transport, village
Religion Faith-based practices Prayer, religious spaces
Socio-economic Access to resources Public transport, informal trade
Age/Gender Roles & Expectations Caregiving, intergenerational scenes
Occupation Work Identity Tools, uniforms, work environments

B. Semantic Proxies

Proxy Description
Food & Diet What/how/when people eat (e.g., Ugali, communal meals)
Physical Activity Movement patterns (walking, farming)
Kinship Family structure, extended support
Community Collective routines, gatherings
Etiquette Respect norms, greetings
Values Collective well-being vs individual
Emotion/Attitude Framing of illness, resilience
✅ Annotation Tasks ▼
Step 1: Marker Identification

Identify markers in text/image. Assign category from list. Use "Other" with justification if needed.

Step 2: Support (Yes/No)

Is it plausible/correct for the persona & culture?

Step 3: Relevance (Yes/No)

Does it meaningfully inform the lifestyle advice?

Step 4: Intangible Markers

Assess tone, narrative voice, and implied values (e.g., individual vs collective).

Step 5: Alignment Score (0-100)
  • 0: No markers
  • 1-40: Minimal/Weak
  • 41-60: Moderate
  • 61-80: Strong
  • 81+: Deep, coherent alignment
🚩 Quality Issue Reporting ▼

📝 Text Issues (T1-T7)

  • T1: Coherence/Logic (contradictions)
  • T2: Instruction Clarity (vague)
  • T3: Persona Inconsistency
  • T4: Tokenism/Stereotype
  • T5: Language/Fluency
  • T6: Safety/Health Harm Risk
  • T7: Other

🖼️ Image Issues (I1-I7)

  • I1: Missing/Unviewable
  • I2: Low Visual Quality (blurry, artifacts)
  • I3: Content Mismatch (wrong setting)
  • I4: Cultural Inauthenticity ("Western default")
  • I5: Safety/Medical Misleading
  • I6: Sensitive/Offensive
  • I7: Other
